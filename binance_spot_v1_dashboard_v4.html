How the strategies are defined and important limitations
  • Spot only: live Binance USDT spot pairs; leveraged tokens are excluded. Each pair opens on TradingView when clicked.
  • EMA 9/21 and EMA 9/33: completed 1H candle; close above EMA 9 after touching/reaching EMA 9 during the candle; EMA 9 above the comparison EMA; EMA 20 above EMA 50 trend filter.
  • Regular bullish divergence: the two most recent confirmed local swing lows show price lower low and RSI higher low.
  • Hidden bullish divergence: the two most recent confirmed local swing lows show price higher low and RSI lower low; 1H EMA 20 above EMA 50 trend filter.
  • +1% target: a displayed calculation from the latest price/entry reference, not a forecast, guaranteed return, or backtest result. Fees and slippage are not included.
  • This first strategy lab identifies candidate setups only. It does not prove any strategy will reach +1%. Use paper testing/backtesting before risking money. It does not place orders and needs no Binance API key.
